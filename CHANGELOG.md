# Changelog

## v1.0 (2026-10-20)

First release.

- Screensaver for Windows 10/11 and macOS 12 or later (Apple Silicon and Intel).
- A Grok Bot face bounces around a black screen and changes shape and color at every edge. It tips in 3D to look where it's heading, blinks, and spins when it hits a corner.
- macOS: frames follow the display's refresh rate (up to 120 Hz on ProMotion) and the face is drawn with Core Animation, which fixes the stutter of earlier test builds.
- The face travels at half the earlier test builds' speed, with the bounce kick to its tilt unchanged.
